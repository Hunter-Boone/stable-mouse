Stable Mouse works with the desktop pointer you already use. Start paused, try a setting, and change it whenever you need.

01 / ADJUST

Find your balance

Choose Light, Balanced, or Strong smoothing. The desktop app also lets you adjust pointer speed. More smoothing reduces shaking but adds delay.

02 / HOLD

Help with small targets

Optional center tracking looks for the middle of repeated back-and-forth shaking. It can help hold a target, but needs several reversals to recognize a pattern.

03 / PAUSE

Stay in charge

Enable or pause from the app or tray. Use the emergency shortcut when you need to. Starting at login and enabling at launch are separate choices.

04 / PRACTICE

Try it at your own pace

Use the built-in practice area to compare settings. No scores, saved movement history, or medical measurements.

About this preview

Pause the desktop app first if it is running. The demo uses the same movement scale as your real cursor. Smoothing adds delay, and reaching an edge can leave the cursors apart. Escape pauses the demo while this section has focus. Your real cursor stays under your control. No movement is saved or uploaded. Browser timing and display scaling can feel different from the app. The app stops remaining drift when you click; the demo keeps that movement so clicks do not leave its two cursors offset.

DEVELOPMENT PREVIEW

Helpful is something we have to test.

This is an early version. Controlled tests show better target holding during regular shaking, but it still adds lag and can struggle with irregular movement. It has not been clinically validated.

Windows has been tested on a workstation with generated input. macOS and Linux builds pass automated checks; physical input testing on those platforms is still pending.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you try it.

Start with a comfortable setting. Pause or quit if it gets in your way.

Is it really free?

Yes. Stable Mouse is free, open-source software under GPL-3.0-only. There is no subscription, account, advertising, or paid tier. You can inspect, modify, and share the code under its license.

Can it help with hand tremor?

It is being built for people who find mouse control difficult because of unwanted hand movement. It reduces some kinds of repeated shaking in generated tests, but usefulness varies. Those results do not establish whether it will help a particular person or condition.

Will the pointer feel delayed?

Yes. Smoothing adds delay, especially at higher strengths. Center tracking can improve steadiness during regular shaking, but does not remove the delay during a clean reach. Try lower smoothing if the pointer feels too slow.

How do I turn it off?

Use Pause in the app or tray, or quit the app. On Windows, press Ctrl + Alt + F8. On macOS, press Control + Option + F8. On Linux, hold the left and right mouse buttons together for two seconds. Escape pauses while the app window has focus.

Does it record my movement?

No. The app does not save movement history or collect analytics. Your preferences stay on your computer. This website adds no analytics, cookies, or third-party scripts; GitHub operates the hosting and download service under its own privacy policy.

Does it work in every app?

It targets ordinary desktop pointer use. Raw-input games, protected administrator windows, remote connections, touchpads, and specialized devices can behave differently or bypass filtering. Read the platform details before relying on it.
